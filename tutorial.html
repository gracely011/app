Gracely Tutorial & Guide

Learn how to install, activate, and enjoy dozens of premium services in one click using Gracely Extension & Gracely Guard.

1

Install Gracely Guard First (Mandatory), Then Gracely Extension

MANDATORY: Install Gracely Guard First

Gracely Guard 1.0.3 is the mandatory system security module that must be installed and enabled first. Without Guard, Gracely Extension cannot activate premium accounts.

Follow this installation sequence in your browser:

  1. Log in to your account and download the extension files from the Gracely Dashboard or go to Manage Extension.
  2. Open your Chrome/Brave/Edge browser and type chrome://extensions in the address bar.
  3. Enable the Developer mode toggle in the top right corner of the page.
  4. Order 1 (Mandatory First): Install gracely-guard-1.0.3.zip.
  5. Order 2: Install gracely-extension-2.2.zip.
Method 1: Drag & Drop (Easiest)

No ZIP extraction needed! Simply drag and drop the gracely-guard-1.0.3.zip file first into chrome://extensions. After Guard is installed, drag and drop gracely-extension-2.2.zip.

Method 2: Extract & Load Unpacked

Extract both ZIP files to your computer, then click Load unpacked in chrome://extensions and select the gracely-guard-1.0.3 folder first, followed by the gracely-extension-2.2 folder.

Don't forget to pin both extension icons to your browser toolbar for quick and easy access.

2

Sign In to Your Gracely Account

The extension will automatically synchronize with your account status on the Gracely website:

  • Open the Gracely Login Page in the same browser.
  • Enter your email and password, then complete the Cloudflare Turnstile verification.
  • Once logged in, the extension will automatically detect your active plan (Premium / Pro / The Phantom) and session tokens.
3

Access Premium Services in One Click

Click the Gracely Extension icon on your browser toolbar:

  • Use the search bar or category filters to find the service you need (e.g. ChatGPT, Netflix, Canva Pro, Coursera, Perplexity, etc.).
  • Click the service button. A new tab will open and your premium session is automatically injected without entering passwords manually.
  • Important: Never click Log Out on target service websites (e.g. Netflix or ChatGPT) to ensure accounts stay active for all users.
4

Tips & Troubleshooting

If Account Does Not Auto-Login or Shows an Error:

1. Open the extension and click the Refresh icon in the top right corner of the extension popup.

2. Clear cache and cookies for the target service, then click the service again from the extension.

3. Ensure Gracely Guard is always in Enabled (Active) status in chrome://extensions.
